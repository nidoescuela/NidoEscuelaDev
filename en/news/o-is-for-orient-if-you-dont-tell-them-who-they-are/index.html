If you don't tell them who they are, the internet will

Nobody leaves a vacuum empty. Your child will receive a view of the world either way. The only question is whose.

NIDO Escuela team · October 6, 2026 · 4 min read

If you don't tell them who they are, the internet will. And it will not wait until they are ready.

The figures

70% of children say sexuality is never discussed at home. The average age of first contact with pornography sits at around eleven and a half.

UNICEF Spain and the University of Santiago de Compostela — survey of more than 75,000 schoolchildren, 2025.

And the figure that hurts most: among children who consume that content, for three in ten those videos are their only source of information about sexuality.

Study on early consumption in Argentina, published by La Nación, 2023.

Now the figure that gives hope. Among children who have adults present in their digital lives, 29% report risk behaviours. Among those without that accompaniment, the number rises to 42%.

UNICEF, UNESCO and Voices — “Kids Online Argentina 2025”.

Thirteen points of difference. That is what one present adult is worth. And it is measurable.

The mirror

Nobody leaves a vacuum empty. If the family does not form identity, something else will: an algorithm, a piece of content, a fashionable narrative, a screen at three in the morning.

We are not debating whether your child will receive a view of the world. They will receive one either way. The only question is whose.

What NIDO does

The O is for ORIENT. To orient is to give true north.

NIDO is a Christian institution, and we say so plainly: we believe the formation of identity, affection and conscience belongs first to the family, and that it holds better from a clear worldview than from noise.

Orienting at NIDO means three concrete things: teaching them to think with their own judgement in the face of any narrative —political, cultural or commercial—; giving them a moral compass that does not shift with the trend; and walking with them toward a personal encounter with Jesus, from which the most solid identity a child can hold is born.

We do not form children isolated from the world. We form children who can stand in the world without being swept along by it.

The foundation

“But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name.”

John 1:12

No screen can give that identity. No trend can take it away.

The invitation

This is why NIDO exists. We nurture what comes in. We ignite what can rise. We develop the whole person. And we orient them toward the One who gives them a name.

It is not an educational method. It is an answer to the urgency of these years.
