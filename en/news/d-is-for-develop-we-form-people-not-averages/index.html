News · N.I.D.O., part three

We ask what grade they got. We almost never ask who they are becoming.

A generation with more information than any before it, and fewer tools to hold themselves up inside. The D in NIDO works where nobody looks.

NIDO Escuela team · September 29, 2026 · 3 min read

We ask children what grade they got. We almost never ask who they are becoming.

The figures

One in seven young people aged 10 to 19 lives with a mental health condition — anxiety and depression leading. In Latin America and the Caribbean that is close to 16 million adolescents.

World Health Organization and UNICEF — estimates on adolescent mental health.

And a newer front: eight out of ten adolescents have accessed online betting or know someone who uses it. Of those, 37% log in very often or every day. 40% say the subject has never been discussed at home.

UNICEF Argentina, 2025.

The mirror

A generation with more information than any other in history, and fewer tools to hold themselves up inside.

They know everything. They don't know how to wait, how to lose, what to do with boredom, what to do with pain. And whatever they cannot handle, they numb: screen, bet, substance, validation.

That is not fixed with more content. It is fixed with character formation.

What NIDO does

The D is for DEVELOP. To develop is to work on the whole person, not the average.

Will. Self-control. Tolerance for frustration. The ability to finish what you start. Real face-to-face relationships. And purpose — because a child with purpose is far harder to buy.

That is why every NIDO student has a Development Profile (PDN): not a report card, but a living map of how that person is growing across every dimension. What gets measured gets cared for.

The foundation

“And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man.”

Luke 2:52

Four dimensions in a single verse: mind, body, spirit and relationships. That is the standard. We invented nothing: we are returning to it.

The invitation

If you can only do one thing this week: ask how they feel before you ask how they did.

And if you want that question to have an institution behind it, we are here.

NIDO. We form people, not averages.

The report card tells one part. The rest can be accompanied too.

The NIDO Development Profile maps how your child is growing across every dimension, not only the ones that fit into a grade. Let's talk about their case.
