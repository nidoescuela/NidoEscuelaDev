Your child will eat three times today. What about their mind?

We obsess over what goes into our children's bodies. We almost never ask what goes into their minds. The N in NIDO starts exactly there.

NIDO Escuela team · September 15, 2026 · 3 min read

Today your child will eat three times. You will check that they drink water, that they don't fill up on sweets, that they eat before bed.

Here is the uncomfortable question: who served breakfast to their mind this morning?

The figure

In Argentina, the average age for a first internet-connected phone is nine and a half. 95% of children aged 9 to 17 already have one. And 46% admit that screen time causes them problems: they sleep worse, perform worse, get angrier.

UNICEF, UNESCO and Voices — “Kids Online Argentina 2025”. National sample of 5,910 children aged 9 to 17, across 20 jurisdictions.

Translated: at nine and a half, a child is handed an open door to every piece of content in the world. No filter, no formed judgement, nobody on the other side explaining what they just saw.

The mirror

No one would hand a nine-year-old the car keys. Yet we hand them something more powerful: a machine that shapes their desire, their self-image and their sense of what is normal, eight hours a day, without a manual.

This is not your fault. It is the air we all breathe. But air can be changed.

What NIDO does

The N is for NURTURE. To nurture is to decide what comes in.

At NIDO we don't manage screens: we build an appetite for something else. A child who discovers they can read, create, argue an idea, finish a project that cost them something, stops needing the infinite scroll to feel alive. Not because we forbade it. Because they found something better.

We nurture with content that has substance, with a human rhythm —not a notification rhythm—, with real relationships, and with spiritual nourishment every day. Because the soul goes hungry too, and that hunger is silent.

The foundation

“Whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely… think on these things.”

Philippians 4:8

It is not a list of prohibitions. It is a diet.

The invitation

This week, one single question at home: what is coming in?

Don't answer it alone. We exist to answer it with you.
