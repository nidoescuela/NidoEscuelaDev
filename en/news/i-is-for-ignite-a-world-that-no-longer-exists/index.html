News · N.I.D.O., part two

We are preparing them for a world that no longer exists

Protecting a child from the world is not enough. You have to launch them into it. The I in NIDO is the difference between containing and igniting.

NIDO Escuela team · September 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Your child will compete for a job that does not exist yet, with tools that have not been invented yet, against people from all over the planet.

And we are preparing them with a school model designed for nineteenth-century factories.

The figures

In Latin America and the Caribbean, an estimated four out of five sixth-grade students fail to reach the minimum level of reading comprehension. The region ranked second worst in the world for learning poverty.

World Bank and UNICEF, with UNESCO — report on learning poverty in Latin America and the Caribbean, 2022.

At the same time: 39% of workplace skills will change or become obsolete before 2030, according to employers across 55 economies.

World Economic Forum — “Future of Jobs Report 2025”.

Put the two figures together and you get the hardest equation of our time: a system that cannot get children reading, preparing them for a world that rewrites itself every eighteen months.

The mirror

Many families arrive at NIDO saying the same thing: “my child isn't the problem, school bores him”. Or the reverse: “my child is falling behind and nobody is stopping it”.

Both sentences say the same thing. The system has one pace and your child has their own.

What NIDO does

The I is for IGNITE. Protecting a child from the world is not enough. You have to launch them into it.

To ignite is to let each child move at their real speed: the one who flies, flies; the one who needs time has it, without the humiliation of being held back. It is accelerated learning with human accompaniment.

It is technology and artificial intelligence used as a tool and not as a crutch — so the child learns to ask the machine the right questions, rather than letting the machine think for them. And it is formal backing: our dual certification exists so that this path has open doors on the other side.

The foundation

“Stir up the gift of God, which is in thee.”

2 Timothy 1:6

Stir up, not hold down. Every child carries a fire. The question is whether the education they receive smothers it or breathes on it.
